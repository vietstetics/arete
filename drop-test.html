Drop test

Checks the camera’s timing and the speed maths against gravity, with no speed device

How to film it

  1. A bright, single-colour ball. A lacrosse ball is best; a tennis ball works. A plain wall behind it in a different colour.
  2. Measure the height. Hold the ball still with its bottom 1–1.5 m above the floor and measure from the floor to the bottom of the ball.
  3. Phone steady and level, 1.5–2 m away, side-on to the drop, with your hand, the whole fall and the floor in the picture.
  4. Normal Video, not Slo-mo, at 60 fps if you can (Settings → Camera → Record Video → 1080p at 60 fps).
  5. Let go, don’t throw, and let it bounce in view. Film 3–5 drops.
