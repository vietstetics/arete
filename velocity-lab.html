3. Arete’s numbers

Mean velocity = D / T: D is how far the weight travelled on rep 1, T each rep’s lifting time, timed between frames. Only counted reps get a velocity. Values below their accuracy tier for this frame rate are marked “estimate”.

4. Reference numbers

From the reference app for this same set, one rep per line: mean velocity (m/s), then optionally peak velocity (m/s), range of motion (cm) and power (W), separated by commas. A CSV export with column names also works.
