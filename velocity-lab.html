Velocity validation

Run a recorded set through Arete and compare its bar speed with a velocity transducer (GymAware, Vitruve, Tendo) or a reference app on the same set. Target: transducer-level agreement on mean velocity — average difference within ±0.03 m/s, RMS ≤ 0.04 m/s, 95% of reps within ±0.06 m/s (and the older ≤ 5% mean error). Protocol: docs/velocity-accuracy.md.

3. Arete’s numbers

3D plate: each lift’s own distance over its own time, from leaving the bottom to stopping at the top (as a transducer times it); every lift gets one unless the plate wasn’t seen where it started or ended. Older trackers: D / T, D from rep 1, counted reps only. Values the camera saw too sparsely or noisily are marked “estimate”.

4. Reference numbers

From the reference app for this same set, one rep per line: mean velocity (m/s), then optionally peak velocity (m/s), range of motion (cm) and power (W), separated by commas. A CSV export with column names also works.
